Alarms anchored to the time zone you meant.

A world clock and alarm app that rings at the right local time, wherever you are.

What it does

  • Set an alarm for a place and it rings at that place’s local time, even when you travel.
  • See world clocks for the cities you care about at a glance.
  • Home Screen widgets and an Apple Watch app show your next alarm with a live countdown.
  • Private by design: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your device.
