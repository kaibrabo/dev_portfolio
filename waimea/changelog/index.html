Changelog

What changed in each release.

1.1.2

Latest

Released 23 September 2026

Added

  • The app asks for a rating, once, after your alarms have rung a few times.

Fixed

  • The confirmation messages at the bottom of the screen stayed in English after you changed language.
  • Sunrise and sunset alarms were not marked Auto in the widget, so a time that moves daily looked like a fixed one changing by itself.

1.1.1

Released 21 September 2026

Added

  • Alarms can follow the sun. Choose Sunrise or Sunset instead of a clock time, and offset it if you like, so an alarm can ring an hour before sunset for golden hour or at first light whatever the season.
  • Sunrise and sunset are worked out for each saved city from its own position, so an alarm pinned to Tokyo follows Tokyo's sun.
  • Solar alarms are marked Auto in your alarm list, since their time moves from day to day.

Fixed

  • Dates appeared as “null” in Chinese, and were wrong in several other languages.
  • Alarm labels stayed in English in notifications, the widget and the full-screen alarm after changing language, although the lists translated them.
  • Cities could only be found by their English name. You can now search in your own language: 東京, 서울, القاهرة, Москва, München or Tokio.

Changed

  • The alarm editor leads with Mode, and shows the offset in place of the clock when you pick a solar alarm.
  • The medium widget has a larger, centred clock, with your place and date on one line beneath it, and a wider column for saved cities so their names fit.
  • Sound names, month names and the remaining buttons now follow the app's language.

1.1.0 was submitted and then withdrawn before release, so it never reached anyone; its changes are included above.

1.0.1

Released

Released 20 September 2026

Fixed

  • On/off switches for saved locations and alarms did not respond. The change was saved but never appeared, so the switch looked stuck.
  • Repeating alarms could stop after ringing once. Only the next occurrence was scheduled, and iOS will not wake a closed app to schedule the one after it.
  • The app now asks for alarm permission on first launch. Previously it only asked if you tapped a banner, so dismissing that banner left alarms unable to ring.
  • Light bars along the top and bottom of every screen.
  • Your home location could not be deleted.

Changed

  • Your current city is shown automatically, named after the nearest major city. Standing in San Rafael now reads as San Francisco rather than Los Angeles.
  • New installs start empty, instead of with a sample city and four alarms to delete.
  • Sheets can be dismissed by tapping outside them, and dim correctly in dark mode.

Added

  • Report a Bug in Settings, which attaches diagnostics to an email so problems can be traced.
  • The version number at the bottom of Settings.

1.0.0

First release

September 2026

Added

  • Alarms anchored to a time zone, so an alarm set for 7:00 AM in Tokyo rings at 7:00 AM in Tokyo no matter where you are.
  • World clocks for saved cities, searchable by city name or airport code.
  • Real system alarms on iOS 26 and later, which ring through silent mode and Focus.
  • Home Screen widgets in three sizes, and an Apple Watch app with a complication.
  • Twelve languages, 12 and 24-hour time, light and dark themes.
  • Everything on device. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.
