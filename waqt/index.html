Waqt

Prayer times you can actually wake up to.

Calculated on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.

What it does

  • The five daily prayers for wherever you are, with your choice of calculation method and madhab.
  • Fajr rings as a real system alarm, through silent mode and Focus, so it actually wakes you.
  • During Ramadan, a suhoor warning before the fast begins and iftar at Maghrib.
  • Everything is computed on your device. There is no account and nothing to sign in to.
